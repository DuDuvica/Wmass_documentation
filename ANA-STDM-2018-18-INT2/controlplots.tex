\section{$\sqrt{s} = 13$~\TeV\ dataset control plots}
\label{subsec:controlplots13}

\todo{Needs update of the text with the final plots}

Control plots for the 13~\TeV\ \lowmu\ dataset are provided after applying the selection described above, applying all object corrections derived for these datasets as well as the physics corrections described in sections~\ref{sec:objects}~and~\ref{sec:modelingCorr}. To better visualise the agreement in the shapes, the MC predictions are normalised to the integral of the data distribution. In each figure, the right(left)-hand column shows distributions for the \Wplus\ (\Wminus) process. The top (bottom) row shows the muon (electron) decay channel. In the ratio panels, the grey band is the total systematic uncertainty, whilst the brown band adds the MC statistical uncertainty in quadrature on top of it, although MC statistics is a tiny contribution everywhere and hardly visible. The systematic uncertainty is the experimental uncertainty, excluding luminosity since the MC is normalised to the data integral. The boson \pt\ modeling uncertainty is also not included. The $\chi^2/\mathrm{ndf}$ shown includes all these uncertainties and is calculated with the full covariance matrix taking into account bin-to-bin correlations of the systematic uncertainties as appropriate.
In regions of the distributions insensitive to the modelling of \ptw\ there is generally good agreement between data and predictions. The bulk of the \mt\ distribution is a typical example of distribution that is mostly insensitive to the modeling of \ptw.
The \ut\ distribution, shown in figure~\ref{fig:ut13_controlplot}, sensitive to \ptw, also shows a decent agreement with the data as is to be expected after
applying the corrections described in Section~\ref{sec:physcorrWpTrew}. It is nonetheless slightly softer than the data in the $W^+$ channel, and this is also reflected in the lepton \pt\
control plots, figure~\ref{fig:ptl13_controlplot}.

\section{$\sqrt{s} = 5$~\TeV\ dataset control plots}

Control plots for the 5~\TeV\ dataset in the same way as for 13~\TeV\ in Subsection~\ref{subsec:controlplots13} with the MC predictions normalised to the integral of the data distribution. As in the 13~\TeV\ situation, the modelling is good and the \ut\ distribution shows also a decent agreement with the data after applying the corrections.
