% Abstract - % directly after { is important for correct indentation
\AtlasAbstract{%

This notes describes the determination of the mass of the $W$ boson in
proton–proton collisions at centre-of-mass energies of $\sqrt{s}=5.02$ TeV and $\sqrt{s}=13$ TeV
with the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider. The data were collected in 2017 and 2018, in
dedicated runs with low pile-up conditions, and correspond
to 255 pb$^{-1}$ and 338 pb$^{-1}$ at $\sqrt{s}=5.02$ TeV and 13
TeV, respectively.  The determination of the $W$ boson mass is done via a profile maximum likelihood fit. 
The fit is performed in the electron and muon channels and separated by charge. The mass is determined via the lepton
\pT and the $W$ \mt\ distributions. 
The value of the $W$ boson mass is XX, which is in 
good/bad agreement to the SM prediction. The uncertainties of this result are dominated by XX.  

}
